- `where_tp` and `obj_tp` are always required. `where_tp` can be one of: "feature_enum", "feature_scalar", "max", "min", "spatial_rel", "and", "or", "not", "true".
- If `where_tp="feature_enum"`, then both `enum_feature` and `enum_param` must also be provided. Example: all tables (`obj_tp`) whose color (`enum_feature`) is brown (`enum_param`).

- If `where_tp="spatial_rel"`, then both `spatial_relation` and `obj_tp2` are needed. Example: all apples (`obj_tp`) that are on top of (`spatial_relation`) a table (`obj_tp2`).

- `where_tp="true"` indicates no constraints.
